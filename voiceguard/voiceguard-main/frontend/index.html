    What to do if you are suspicious

    Act straight away
    Never share secrets

    Do not give OTPs, PINs, bank details or passwords to a caller, whatever they say.

    Hang up and verify

    Scammers invent urgency and authority. Hang up and call back on an official number you already trust.

    Report quickly

    In India, call 1930 or file a report at cybercrime.gov.in.

    Results are automated risk indicators, not proof. When in doubt, hang up and verify.
